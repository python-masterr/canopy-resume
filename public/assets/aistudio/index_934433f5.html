    From a pile of PDFs to a short list

    Four steps, each one a little taller than the last.

    Read

    Canopy reads each resume and pulls out contact details, sections, years of experience and results.

    Detect

    Skills are matched against a library of about 60 tools, including aliases like k8s for Kubernetes.

    Rank

    Each resume gets a score out of 100 with a breakdown, and the pool is ranked.

    Grow

    Canopy suggests jobs that fit the skills found, and the skills to learn to qualify for them.

    Questions

    How is the score calculated?

    Out of 100: skills breadth 30, resume sections 20, experience 15, measurable results 15, action verbs 10 and contact info 10.

    How are skill gaps found?

    Canopy looks at the areas where a resume is strongest, such as frontend or cloud, and lists related skills that are missing from it.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. Screening runs entirely in this page, so resumes never leave your device.

    Can it replace a human reviewer?

    No. It is a first pass that surfaces evidence. Keyword matching can miss skills described in other words, so review the short list yourself.

    How are job suggestions made?

    Each role has a core skill set. Canopy compares it with the skills on the resume and shows the three closest roles, with the missing skills as your learning list.
